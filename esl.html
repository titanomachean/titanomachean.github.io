Lesson 1: The Language of Hypotheses (Stranger Things)

"And if I only could, I'd make a deal with God, and I'd get him to swap our places..."

Grammar Breakdown / Explicación

Español: Cuando expresas una situación imaginaria o imposible en el presente ("Si tan solo pudiera..."), en inglés formal se usa el Segundo Condicional. La regla dicta usar If + Pasado Simple, seguido por Would + Verbo Base.

English: Kate Bush sings "I'd make a deal" (I would make). Even though she is talking about the present moment, she uses the past tense structure to signal that the situation is completely imaginary.
